Request Account & Data Deletion

This page provides official instructions for users of Chugli to permanently delete their account and associated personal data, including options for users who have already uninstalled the application or cannot access their mobile device.

1. What Data Is Deleted Upon Account Deletion?

When your account deletion is processed, our system executes an immediate, irreversible database hard-delete that cascades across all records. The following data is permanently wiped forever:

  • Authentication Credentials: Your phone number and authentication record in Supabase Auth (auth.users).
  • User Profile: Your anonymous username handle, phone reference, country calling code, language preference, and privacy settings.
  • User Content: All whispers (posts) authored by you.
  • Comments: All replies and comments posted by your account.
  • Interactions: All likes you have placed on other whispers.
  • Safety Records: Your blocked accounts list, moderation reports submitted by you, and moderation reports targeting your posts.
  • Rate Limiting Counters: Any abuse-prevention bucket records associated with your account.
  • Device Storage: All cached tokens, posts, and session keys are wiped from local device storage upon deletion.
⚠️ Permanent & Irreversible: Deletion is permanent in active databases, with no user grace period or soft-delete holding period. Once your account is deleted, your handle and posts cannot be recovered.

2. Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Chugli application installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the Chugli app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar (or your user badge in the top header).
  3. Scroll down to the Account Danger Zone card.
  4. Tap Permanently Delete Account.
  5. Review the confirmation dialog and tap Delete Forever.

Your account will be deleted on the server immediately, your session will be signed out, and you will be returned to the initial onboarding screen.

3. Method 2: Manual Deletion Request via Email (Without App Installed)

If you have uninstalled the Chugli app, lost access to your device, or are unable to delete your account in-app, you can submit a manual deletion request to our administrative team.

Please note: Chugli accounts are registered and authenticated strictly via phone number, not email address. Because of this, an incoming email alone cannot prove ownership of an account.

Step 1: Send an email to: rsquarehub2802@gmail.com

Step 2: Use the subject line: [Account Deletion Request] - Registered Phone Number

Step 3: In the body of your email, please provide:

  • Your registered phone number with international country calling code (e.g. +91 99999 00001).
  • Your registered anonymous handle (optional, if remembered).
  • A clear statement confirming that you authorize the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.

4. Verification & Manual Processing

To protect users against fraudulent or unauthorized account deletions, requests submitted via email undergo manual review:

  • Phone Verification: An authorized administrator will contact your registered phone number directly to verify that the deletion request was genuinely authorized by the phone number owner before any records are removed.
  • Manual Dashboard Deletion: Following successful verification, an authorized administrator manually deletes the account through the Supabase Dashboard. This immediately triggers the database cascade (ON DELETE CASCADE) that permanently removes your authentication record, profile, whispers, comments, likes, reports, and rate-limit counters.
  • Email Confirmation: The administrator will reply to your email once the manual database deletion has been completed.
  • Active Operational Deletion: Once account deletion is completed, personal data and associated interaction records are deleted from active operational databases. Copies that may exist in routine backups or system logs may remain for a limited period and are handled according to the applicable retention policies of those systems.
